When the people closest to the asset are locking it up while the price bleeds, that is the floor forming.

The discipline check is the gate count, and it is the reason this is a 10% buy and not a 25% buy. We hold at three of nine gates — value, the new ETF-outflow signal, and holder conviction — but we do not yet have weekly RSI under 30, seven sustained days of sub-15 sentiment, or a confirmed macro turn, and the 200-week moving average is now contested rather than the clean support it was on May 28. Phase 1B (the next 15%) needs two more gates — most plausibly a sub-30 weekly RSI and either a sustained sub-15 F&G or a confirmed macro turn at the FOMC. Until then, 90% of the book earns ~4.4% in T-bills while the staking lock-up does the patient work of tightening float beneath the price.
