## 11. Strategic Verdict

**Adjusted Score:** 9 / 20 · **Weighted EV:** $82.13 (−1.0% vs spot) · **Sentiment:** Fear (ASI 35 / F&G 33) · **Stance:** **PREPARE — no deployment. 100% dry powder.**

Solana presents the framework's classic trap: the single most attractive *valuation* reading of the four assets — down nearly 72% from its January-2025 peak, which maxes the valuation category at 5/5 — paired with the weakest *confirmation*. This is exactly the situation the gate system exists to police. A 72% drawdown tells you the asset is cheap relative to its own history; it does not tell you the knife has landed. And the evidence says it hasn't: SOL trades roughly 23% below a 200-day moving average that is itself sloping down, there has been no capitulation liquidation flush, funding is only mildly negative with no confirmed streak, and a steady FTX-estate supply drip (~$16–17M/month through 2028) caps every rebound attempt. Two gates of nine is not a bottom; it's a falling knife that merely *looks* cheap.

What keeps SOL from being a pure avoid is the structural floor forming underneath the price: ~67–68% of supply is staked and locked, the spot-ETF complex just crossed $1B cumulative with inflows turning back up after six down months, and the $80 range-support has held for four months. Those are the ingredients of an eventual base — but they are necessary, not sufficient. The framework wants to see either a *deeper* flush that finally prints capitulation (taking the score's gates up via a liquidation spike and extreme sentiment) or a *trend repair* (reclaiming the 200-day, flipping gate 6 and confirming sustained ETF inflows). Neither has happened. Buying here would be paying for cheapness without confirmation — and SOL's 1.5–2× beta to Bitcoin means that if BTC tests its own $68–72K zone, SOL likely sees the $60s first.

The right posture is patient preparation: keep 100% dry powder, pre-write the Phase 1A ticket, and let one of two triggers do the work. Cash earns ~4.4% while the chart decides.
