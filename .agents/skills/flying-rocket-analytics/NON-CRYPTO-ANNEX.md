## Adapted Non-Crypto Reads (OUT OF DECLARED SCOPE) — annex, 2026-08-05

**Equity indices and metals are NOT in this framework's scope, and this annex does not put them in it.** The `description`, the Overview asset-scope line and the Asset Generalization table in the main SKILL are unchanged and remain crypto-only. This annex exists because such reports get requested anyway — five have been produced (gold ×2, SPX ×2, and the gold pair's companions) — and the ad hoc regime demonstrably drifted: four reports, four gate denominators, two rounding conventions, and two mutually contradictory valuation readings on the same asset on the same day. **The annex formalizes the restrictions, not the coverage.** Every rule below is a tightening or a disclosure; none makes a non-crypto short easier than the ad hoc practice it replaces.

A non-crypto read carries the **ADAPTED / OUT OF SCOPE banner** in its header, flags every substitution at the point of use, and — exactly as for smaller alts — **requires explicit user acknowledgement before any deployment number is produced.** No phase may unlock on an adapted read without it.

**1. Frozen gate schema.** N/A = **gates 4 (perp funding), 6 (Coinbase Premium), 9 (BTC-dominance / altseason rotation)** → **active denominator 6**, for BOTH the equity-index and metals classes. Gates 4 and 6 have no instrument in any market state. Gate 9 is an intra-crypto rotation construct whose only non-crypto substitute — sector or safe-haven leadership — restates gate 8's breadth logic rather than adding independent evidence, and a substitute that double-counts another active gate is not a substitute; it is a free gate. Doubt about a substitute's *independence* resolves to N/A; doubt about a gate's *measurability* resolves to ⚠️-and-active (a merely unmeasured gate is never folded into the denominator). Encoded as `FR_NONCRYPTO_NA`; the linter errors on deviation from 2026-08-05.

**2. Valuation Extreme — drift-adjusted ceiling.** The ATH-distance fallback pays the leg's full **5/5** for "at a new high," which for an asset class with structural upward drift is a base-rate condition, not a valuation extreme: an equity index makes new highs on the order of one session in fourteen, against MVRV-Z >5 on ~2–4% of BTC days. The fallback therefore **caps at 3** for non-crypto classes, *unless* an independently sourced long-run valuation measure is at or above its **90th percentile versus ≥50 years of its own history**, in which case the fallback's own band stands. Pinned measures: equity indices → **Shiller CAPE**; metals → **CPI-deflated real price**. Print the measure, its value, its percentile, the **history window**, the source and the as-of date. **Unsourced, or staler than one quarter → the cap at 3 applies** (unsourced resolves toward harder-to-short, mirroring the correlation sourcing rule). The ceiling can never raise the leg above what the fallback alone would score, in any state.

*Worked, on the 2026-08-04 SPX tape:* CAPE **41.9** (Aug 1 2026; long-run mean ~17, record 44.2) sits above its 90th percentile on any plausible ≥50y window, so the ATH band of 5 **stands** — the leg was not over-credited in substance that day, only by accident of mechanism. In a median-CAPE regime the same record high scores **3**, not 5.

**3. Untransferable thresholds resolve to NOT MET.** Where a leg or gate threshold is denominated in a crypto-native absolute unit — gate 7 and Distribution sub-leg (a)'s ">$500M/day, pro-rate by mcap" — and the substitution cannot honour the pro-rating, the leg or gate is **NOT MET**. NOT MET means **❌ and ACTIVE**, never N/A: the denominator is preserved, which is the harder-to-short direction and keeps this rule from colliding with §1 above. The underlying evidence remains admissible in §9 prose and in the bounded S1 term — this quarantines a broken threshold, it does not destroy information. **Corollary: per-day rates are computed on CALENDAR days**, because the crypto reference market that calibrated the bar trades 365 days a year. (`$77.6B / 181 = $429M/day`, which fails the >$500M bar; the same figure on ~129 trading days reads `$602M/day` and passes. That is exactly the ✅/❌ flip, and it was taken the wrong way once already.)

**4. Rounding is pinned, not declared.** `spx` / `sp500` / `ndx` / `nasdaq` → **half-down**, which resolves a .5 away from an unlock on this side of the book (and away from a deploy on the Fallen Knives side — conservative on both, which is why the shared table is safe to pin). A report may no longer override a pinned convention; the linter errors from 2026-08-05.

**5. Correlation suppressor, when the asset IS the benchmark.** For an equity index, the sourced 30-day correlation *to equities* is 1.00 by construction and carries no information. Declare the risk-on suppressor **structurally ON** while price is within 3% of the ATH — the §4A conjunct as written, not "the 1-year high" — and record the degeneracy rather than a measurement: the machine block writes `corr: null` with `corr_degenerate: true`, never `1.000`, because `exports/signal-feed.json` is a contract a downstream consumer reads. **This may never operate as an off-switch.** Outside the 3% band the existing sourced-or-default-ON rule governs unchanged. Note also that one fact — proximity to the high — is doing double duty across the Valuation leg and the gate floor; that is permitted in this direction, but state it.

**6. Channel B is UNAVAILABLE.** Channel B gate 8 (funding not sustained-negative) is the only gate in either framework that voids an unlock on its own, §4B forbids ever marking it N/A, and there is no funding instrument on a cash index or on bullion. That leaves only a vacuous ✅ — silently disabling one of Hard Rule 6's seven never-relax items — or unscorability. It is **unscorable**: no non-crypto Channel B read may be produced.

**7. Phase 3 is UNREACHABLE.** With Distribution sub-leg (a) and Vulnerability sub-leg (a) both structurally absent, those legs cap at 2/3 each, so the maximum attainable mechanical score is **5 + 4 + 5 + 2 + 2 = 18**, against a Phase 3 line of 19. This is codified rather than left as an accident, so that no future report "repairs" the ceiling with a generous substitute. Phases 1A / 1B / 2 remain reachable — the rubric is restricted, not decorative.

**8. Two protections are inert, and must be printed as inert.** The **squeeze-trap penalty** is defined entirely on perp funding and is therefore a **MISSING PROTECTION**, not `penalty: 0` — a `0` records "measured, none present," which is a false statement about an unmeasurable input, and the omission runs in the short-easier direction with no substitute pinned (short interest, hard-to-borrow status and skew inversion all exist and none is calibrated here). The **carry veto** also cannot fire: an index short's carry is `r − q + borrow`, structurally *income* while the risk-free rate exceeds the dividend yield, and the mandatory zero-floor caps income's contribution at zero, so "carry > 40% of target" is unreachable. State the carry decomposition in full anyway.

**9. Mandatory disclosure line.** Every adapted non-crypto report names **which of Hard Rule 6's seven never-relax items are inert or unevaluable in that run**, and why. On an equity index that is at minimum the funding veto gate (§6) and the carry veto (§8). The report must still print the canonical phase-tag registry even when no tranche fills; if a supported derivative expression is actually opened, its `FR-A-*` / `FR-B-*` / `FR-S*` tag is applied exactly as on crypto. If the current position ledger has no counterpart for the derivative, **position reconciliation and size-cap enforcement** remain impaired until that contract is mapped — but the derivative is not untaggable and the report must not claim that it is. A framework that cannot enforce four of its seven untouchables on an asset class should say so in every report on that asset class.

**10. Standing N=1 caveat — print it verbatim, until it expires.** Every adapted non-crypto report carries this line:

> **N=1, and weaker than usual:** four reports, zero trades, zero realized P&L on this asset class — and Flying Rocket is still N=0 on realized money on its native class. Re-grade at the first non-crypto report that reaches a non-stand-down score; there has never been one.

**Retirement condition.** The caveat is **self-expiring**. It is retired by the first adapted non-crypto report whose **adjusted score reaches the Channel A Phase 1A line of 11** — whether or not the gates, the §7 preflight or the minimum-edge filter then veto the trade. That threshold is the trigger because it is the first moment these substitutions bear weight on a decision instead of being academic: below it every non-crypto read has been a stand-down that the rubric could not have gotten wrong in the expensive direction.

The report that trips it must, in the same session: (a) state that the caveat's condition has fired and that this is the class's first non-stand-down score; (b) **delete this item 10 from this annex** and record the deletion in the Framework Revision Log with the triggering report's filename; and (c) **run `framework-calibration` on this framework before any tranche on that asset fills.** A first score is evidence that the adaptation is live, not evidence that it is calibrated.

**What expires is the caveat, and nothing else.** Items 1–9 are restrictions, not disclosures, and they survive the retirement untouched — the frozen schema, the valuation ceiling, the pinned rounding, the NOT-MET rule, Channel B's unavailability, Phase 3's unreachability, the two inert protections and the Hard-Rule-6 disclosure line all remain in force. Deleting the annex, or any part of it other than this item, is a loosening and is out of scope for the report that trips the trigger.
